
SMARTCART E-COMMERCE STORE
DEMO CUSTOMER POLICIES

1. RETURN POLICY

Customers can request a return within 7 days
after receiving a delivered product.

Products must be unused and returned in their
original packaging.

Products damaged by customers are not eligible
for a return under this demo policy.

2. REFUND POLICY

Refund requests are accepted for eligible
delivered orders.

The customer must provide a valid order ID.

The order must be verified before a refund
can be processed.

A refund requires customer confirmation.

Refund amounts are based on the original
order amount recorded in the database.

3. SHIPPING POLICY

Orders are processed after successful placement.

Processing orders have not yet been dispatched.

Shipped orders are on their way to the customer.

Delivered orders have been marked as received.

4. CANCELLATION POLICY

Customers may request cancellation before
an order is dispatched.

Orders that have already been shipped or
delivered cannot be cancelled under this
demo policy.

5. PRODUCT AVAILABILITY

Product availability depends on the stock
quantity recorded in the store database.

When stock is zero, the product is out of stock.

When stock is greater than zero, the product
is available for purchase.
